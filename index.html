WebアプリケーションWeb applications

予約、顧客、在庫、社内の申請。紙やスプレッドシートの業務を、使いやすいアプリへ。Bookings, customers, stock, internal requests. Move paper and spreadsheets into an app people enjoy.

業務の自動化Workflow automation

繰り返しの入力や通知を自動化。人にしかできない仕事に、時間を戻します。Automate repeated entry and notifications, and give time back to work only people can do.
